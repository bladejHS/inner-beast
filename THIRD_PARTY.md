# Third-party ideas

Activități, Jurnal and Monitor were written from scratch for this app; no source code was copied.
They follow the feature sets and documented methods of these projects:

- **Endurain** (AGPL-3.0): GPX/TCX activities, statistics, gear tracking. "Endurain" is a trademark of its project; this app is not affiliated with it.
- **SparkyFitness** (see its LICENSE, copyright codewithcj): check-ins, measurements, symptoms, medication, goals.
- **NOOP** (PolyForm Noncommercial 1.0.0): the Charge and Effort concepts, built here from the published methods its docs cite (Task Force 1996, Karvonen, Edwards, Tanaka). Not affiliated with WHOOP.

Not medical software.
